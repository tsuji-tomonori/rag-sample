# Literature-based architecture review

1. State the quality scenario, failure mode, and measurable acceptance condition.
2. Prefer original papers and official provider specifications over secondary summaries.
3. Record alternatives, assumptions, limitations, and operational cost in an ARC.
4. Keep dataset-specific answers out of production logic and verify decisions with evaluation artifacts.
5. Revisit RRF constants, chunking, thresholds, reranking, and models when evaluation evidence changes.
